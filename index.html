About DragonTV

DragonTV is a media player designed for Smart TVs. It allows users to connect their own legally authorized IPTV sources using supported playlist and account formats such as M3U and Xtream Codes.

DragonTV does not provide, host, sell, or distribute TV channels, playlists, subscriptions, movies, series, or other media content.

Main Features

  • Support for M3U and M3U8 playlists
  • Support for Xtream Codes
  • EPG support when provided by the user's service
  • TV remote friendly navigation
  • Full-screen media playback
  • Multiple playlist support

Legal Notice

This application is only a media player. Users must provide their own legally authorized IPTV source and are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights and permissions to access any content used with the application.

DragonTV is not affiliated with or endorsed by any third-party IPTV provider.
